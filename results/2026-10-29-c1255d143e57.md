# Competitor benchmark results

Method: criterion 0.5, 3 s measurement, 1 s warm-up, 100 samples
(spec section 11.3), pinned with `taskset -c 9`.

## Note

The machine ran other work during this run (several fuzzers; load
average 6 to 12), and pinning keeps only this process on its core. Every
time, on both sides, is about half higher than in a quiet run, so compare
rows within a group, not with other files.

`hyper/...` row of the same group to estimate hyper's own cost.
